FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions participants ask most often.

How do I join an event?

Browse the upcoming events on the home page, click the event card, this will take you to the event details page where you can register. Once registered the event will show under the 'Registered' tab of the home page. Make sure to register before an event starts

How do I create or join a team?

Navigate to the Teams section inside your event by clicking the event's card in the homepage. From there you can create a new team or join an existing one.

 How do I submit my solution?

Click the event card in home page, open the Submissions tab, upload your source archive and solution output, then submit before the event deadline.

 Can I submit multiple times?

Yes. You may upload new submissions until the submission deadline. Your latest valid submission for each level is used for the final results.

 How are submissions graded?

The platform automatically evaluates your submission using the organizer's solver and generates a score along with a feedback log.

 Where can I view my submission history?

Open your event, navigate to the History page and view your submissions
