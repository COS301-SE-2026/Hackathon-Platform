Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions participants ask most often.

How do I register for an event?

Browse the Upcoming or Ongoing events on the home page and select an event to view its details. From there, you can register to participate. Once registered, the event will appear under the Registered tab on your home page.

How do I create or join a team?

Once you're registered click on the event and navigate to the Team tab. From there, you can create a new team or request to join an existing one.

How do I submit my solution?

Open your event and navigate to the Submissions tab. If the organiser has enabled the built-in IDE, click "Open IDE" to write and submit your code directly through the platform. Otherwise, upload your source archive and solution output through the Submissions tab. Make sure to submit before the event deadline.

Where can I view my submission history?

Open your event and navigate to the History page to review your previous submissions, scores and feedback.

Where can I view the leaderboard?

Open your event and navigate to the Rankings to view team rankings and compare scores.

Where can I find the event's announcements?

Open your event and navigate to the Announcements tab to view updates and important information shared by the organiser.
