FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions participants ask most often.

How do I join an event?

Browse the open events and click the Register button before the registration deadline.Once accepted, the event will appear in your Active Events carousel.

 How do I create or join a team?

Navigate to the Teams section inside your event by clicking the View Team button. From there you can create a new team or join an existing one.

 How do I submit my solution?

Click Go to Event, open the Submissions tab, upload your source archive and solution output, then submit before the event deadline.

 Can I submit multiple times?

Yes. You may upload new submissions until the submission deadline. Your latest valid submission for each level is used forthe final results.

 How are submissions graded?

The platform automatically evaluates your submission using the organizer's solver and generates a score along with a feedback log.

 Where can I view my submission history?

Open your event, navigate to the Submissions page, and scroll down to view your submission history.
