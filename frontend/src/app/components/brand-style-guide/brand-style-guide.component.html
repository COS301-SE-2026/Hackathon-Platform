Brand Foundation

T

Purpose

"To build the most intuitive and reliable platform for hackathon organizers and participants."

We exist to remove friction from hackathon management. Oragnizers should focus on running great events, not wrestling software.

D

Values

  • ClarityEvery screen , button , and message should be immediately understood.
  • ReliabilityThe platform should work consistently, without surprises.
  • DelightSmall moments of polish that make users smile.
V

Voice

Confident, calm, and helpful

We are the expert guide who never talks down. We explain clearly, we anticipate needs, and we stay calm when things go wrong.

Accessibility Standards

The floor, not the ceiling.

E

Perceivable

Can users sense the content?

  • WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large)
  • Alt text on meaningful images
  • Don't rely on colour alone
K

Operable

Can users drive the interface?

  • Keyboard navigable
  • Visible focus rings
  • No keyboard traps
U

Understandable

Can users predict behaviour?

  • Consistent navigation
  • Plain-language errors
  • Proper form labels
R

Robust

Will it work everywhere?

  • Test with screen readers
  • Semantic HTML
  • ARIA where needed

Voice & Tone

Principles

  • Be Clear Say what you mean, mean what you say
  • Be Brief Delete unnecessary words
  • Be Human Write like a person, not a robot
  • Be Helpful Solve problems, don't just report them
