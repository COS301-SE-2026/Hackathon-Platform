Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We've got answers

Everything you need to know before joining or hosting your next hackathon.

Do I need to be an expert to participate?

No. Hackathons are open to all skill levels, from beginners to experienced developers.

 Can I participate without a team?

Yes. You can compete solo, create your own team, or request to join an existing team.

 How are submissions scored?

Submissions are evaluated using the official solver supplied by the event organizer.

 Can I join private hackathons?

Yes. Private hackathons require an invitation or registration key provided by the organizer.

 Can organizations host hackathons?

Absolutely. Organizers can create events, manage teams, upload resources, and monitor submissions from the admin portal.
