Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before joining or hosting your next hackathon.

Do I need coding experience to participate?

Not necessarily. Experience requirements depend on the hackathon. Check the event details to see whether it's suitable for your skill level.

Can I participate without a team?

You can create your own team or request to join an existing one. Whether solo participation is allowed depends on the event's rules.

Can I join a hackathon after it has started?

Yes. You can still register and participate after the event has started, as long as it hasn't ended.

Do I have to use the built-in IDE?

No. The built-in IDE is optional and only available when enabled by the organiser. You can develop your solution using external tools and submit it through the platform.

 Can I submit my solution more than once?

Yes. You can submit your solution as many times as you like before the event ends, allowing you to improve your solution and achieve a higher score.

How are submissions scored?

Submissions are automatically evaluated using the solver configured by the organiser. Each submission receives a numerical score based on how well the solution meets the challenge's evaluation criteria.

How are teams ranked on the leaderboard?

Teams are ranked by their scores, with higher scores appearing first. If two teams have the same score, the team whose qualifying submission was received first ranks higher.

Will I receive a certificate after participating?

Yes. certificates are issued by organisers and can include a QR code for verification.

 Can I organise multiple events under one hackathon?

Yes. You can create multiple events under a single hackathon, with the same problem statement, levels, starter resources and solver shared across those events.

 Can organisers manage events after they've started?

Yes. Organisers can update event details, extend timers, manage teams, publish announcements and monitor event activity while an event is running.
