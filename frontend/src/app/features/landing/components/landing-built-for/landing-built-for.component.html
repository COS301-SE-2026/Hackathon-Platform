THE CHALLENGE

Hackathons should be exciting, not complicated.

Hackathons are about creativity, collaboration and innovation but managing multiple tools and complicated processes can make hackathons harder than they need to be.

For Participants

Building something great is challenging enough without juggling multiple platforms.

  • Finding hackathons and forming teams across different platforms.
  • Switching between tools to collaborate and develop solutions.
  • Navigating confusing submission processes.
  • Tracking scores, feedback and results across platforms.

For Organisers

Running a hackathon shouldn't mean managing everything across disconnected tools.

  • Managing registrations, teams and events across platforms.
  • Coordinating communication and tracking submissions.
  • Handling time-consuming scoring and evaluations.
  • Keeping participants informed and monitoring event activity.

That's why we built Hackathon Platform. One platform. Fewer obstacles.
